Pade approximants: rational P(x)/Q(x) that beats Taylor, especially near poles

  exp(x): degree-8 Taylor vs [4/4] Pade (both use 9 coefficients):

      x   Taylor(8) error   Pade [4/4] error
      1          3.06e-06           1.10e-07
      2          1.75e-03           1.67e-04
      3          7.64e-02           2.01e-02
      4          1.17e+00           8.71e-01
      5          1.01e+01           1.98e+01
    -> the Taylor error grows fast; the Pade approximant stays tight.

  f(x) = 1/((1-x)(1+2x)) has poles at x=1 and x=-0.5; Taylor radius is 0.5:

       x    Taylor error    Pade error
    0.30        4.19e-03      1.11e-16
    0.45        1.35e-01      1.11e-16
    0.70        5.69e+00      2.22e-15
    0.90        4.59e+01      5.15e-14
    -> past x=0.5 the Taylor series blows up; the Pade approximant tracks the function
    because its denominator reproduces the poles.
