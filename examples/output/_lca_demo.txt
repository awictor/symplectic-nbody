Lowest common ancestor by binary lifting: O(log n) per query

  A 10-node org tree rooted at CEO. Precomputed 2^k-ancestor table, then:

             u             v          LCA  dist
       Mgr-API      Mgr-Data   Dir-Backend     2
       Mgr-API  Dir-Frontend       VP-Eng     3
      Mgr-Data      Mgr-West          CEO     6
       Mgr-API      Mgr-West          CEO     6
  Dir-Frontend      VP-Sales          CEO     3

  k-th ancestor of Mgr-API (node 7):
    0 up -> Mgr-API
    1 up -> Dir-Backend
    2 up -> VP-Eng
    3 up -> CEO

  Path from Mgr-API to Mgr-West (jump one step at a time):
    Mgr-API -> Dir-Backend -> VP-Eng -> CEO -> VP-Ops -> Dir-Field -> Mgr-West

  dist(u,v) = depth[u] + depth[v] - 2*depth[lca]. The whole path routes through the LCA.
