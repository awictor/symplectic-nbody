Gale-Shapley stable matching: applicants <-> schools

  applicant-proposing (applicant-optimal) matching:
    Ada  -> Yale   (applicant's #2 choice, school's #1 choice)
    Bo   -> UCLA   (applicant's #3 choice, school's #1 choice)
    Cy   -> NYU    (applicant's #4 choice, school's #1 choice)
    Di   -> MIT    (applicant's #2 choice, school's #1 choice)

  stable (no blocking pair): True
  number of stable matchings: 1

  the stable matching is unique: applicant- and school-optimal coincide.

  Deferred acceptance: each free applicant proposes to their next-favourite school; the
  school tentatively holds its best offer so far and rejects the rest. Since schools only
  ever trade up and applicants only ever move down their list, it ends in <= n^2 proposals
  with a provably stable result -- the mechanism behind real residency and school matches.
