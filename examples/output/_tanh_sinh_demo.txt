Tanh-sinh (double-exponential) quadrature: taming endpoint singularities

  integrand                             exact      tanh-sinh        Simpson
  x^2                              0.33333333     0.33333333     0.33333333
  exp(x)                           1.71828183     1.71828183     1.71828183
  1/sqrt(x)  (blows up at 0)       2.00000000     1.99999999     1.99109949
  ln(1/x)  (log blowup at 0)       1.00000000     1.00000000     0.99979276
  1/sqrt(1-x^2)  (blows up at 1)   1.57079633     1.57079631     1.56450271
  1/sqrt(x(1-x))  (both ends)      3.14159265     3.14159262     3.12379161

  On smooth integrands both methods nail it; on the singular ones tanh-sinh stays
  accurate to ~1e-8 while naive Simpson loses digits (its endpoint sample sits on or near
  the blow-up). The substitution x = tanh(pi/2 sinh t) clusters abscissae exponentially
  toward the endpoints without ever reaching them, and the transformed integrand decays
  double-exponentially, so a plain trapezoid rule in t converges with astonishing speed.
