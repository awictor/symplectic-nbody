Debye shielding: lambda_D = sqrt(eps0 kT / n e^2); plasma if N_D >> 1

           environment   n (/m^3)    T (K)    lambda_D       N_D        f_p
  -------------------------------------------------------------------------
     solar wind (1 AU)      5e+06    1e+05       9.8 m   1.9e+10     20 kHz
            ionosphere      1e+12    1e+03     2.18 mm   4.4e+04    9.0 MHz
          solar corona      1e+15    2e+06     3.09 mm   1.2e+08  283.9 MHz
    lab (tokamak edge)      1e+18    1e+05     0.02 mm   4.4e+04    9.0 GHz
           fusion core      1e+20    1e+08     0.07 mm   1.4e+08   89.8 GHz

  Below the plasma frequency, EM waves are reflected. The ionosphere's
  ~9 MHz cutoff is why AM radio (~1 MHz) bounces around the curve of the
  Earth while FM (~100 MHz) and TV punch straight through to space. The
  Debye length is the screening radius, and with thousands of particles
  inside a Debye sphere the shielding is a smooth collective effect.
