  perm(A) = sum over permutations of prod A[i][sigma(i)] -- the determinant without signs.
  That missing sign makes it #P-complete (harder than NP), but Ryser's inclusion-exclusion
  over the 2^n column subsets, iterated in Gray-code order, computes it in O(2^n n) -- the
  method behind bipartite matching counts and boson-sampling amplitudes.
