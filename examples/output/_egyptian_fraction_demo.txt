Egyptian fractions: every rational as a sum of distinct unit fractions

  greedy Fibonacci-Sylvester decompositions:
    2/3 = 1/2 + 1/6
    3/7 = 1/3 + 1/11 + 1/231
    5/6 = 1/2 + 1/3
    4/13 = 1/4 + 1/18 + 1/468
    5/121 = 1/25 + 1/757 + 1/763309 + 1/873960180913 + 1/1527612795642093418846225
    2/2011 = 1/1006 + 1/2023066

  Engel expansions (x = 1/a1 + 1/(a1 a2) + ..., a non-decreasing):
    3/7: a = [3, 4, 7]
    5/12: a = [3, 4]
    7/15: a = [3, 3, 5]

  Sylvester's sequence (each term = 1 + product of all previous):
    [2, 3, 7, 43, 1807, 3263443]
    sum of reciprocals races toward 1:
      1 terms: 1/2  (1 - 1/2)
      2 terms: 5/6  (1 - 1/6)
      3 terms: 41/42  (1 - 1/42)
      4 terms: 1805/1806  (1 - 1/1806)
      5 terms: 3263441/3263442  (1 - 1/3263442)

  The greedy numerator strictly shrinks each step (Fibonacci's 1202 termination proof),
  so every fraction in (0,1) has a finite Egyptian expansion with distinct denominators.
