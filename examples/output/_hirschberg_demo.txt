  sequence A: AGGTCACGTA  (len 10)
  sequence B: AGCTACGCA  (len 9)

  optimal global alignment (match +1, mismatch -1, gap -1):
    AGGTCACGTA
    || | ||| |
    AGCT-ACGCA

  score 4 (full Needleman-Wunsch agrees: True)
  longest common subsequence: AGCACGA

  divide-and-conquer: split A at its midpoint (position 5); the optimal alignment
  crosses B at column 4, so it recurses on A[:5]xB[:4] and A[5:]xB[4:].

  memory: full matrix needs 110 cells; Hirschberg keeps only two rows = 20.
  For genome-scale inputs this is the difference between gigabytes and kilobytes, at the
  cost of only a constant factor more time -- the same optimal alignment, linear space.
