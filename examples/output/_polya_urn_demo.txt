Polya urn -- reinforcement: the rich get richer, the limit is random
====================================================================

Start: 2 black, 3 white. Each draw returns the ball plus 1 of its colour.
Martingale value E[black fraction] = a/(a+b) = 0.400 at every step.

Ten runs freeze into DIFFERENT stable fractions (each fixed by early luck):
   run    final black fraction
   ------------------------------
     1    0.319
     2    0.355
     3    0.331
     4    0.250
     5    0.369
     6    0.223
     7    0.218
     8    0.036
     9    0.122
    10    0.578
  spread: 0.036..0.578 -- no single limit, unlike Ehrenfest

Limiting distribution is Beta(2, 3):
  mean:     empirical 0.3999   Beta 0.4000
  variance: empirical 0.0398   Beta 0.0400

Exchangeability -- any ordering of the same draws is equally likely:
  P(BWB) = 0.08571
  P(BBW) = 0.08571
  P(WBB) = 0.08571
  (de Finetti: this is exactly what makes the limit a random Beta frequency.)
