Top Trading Cycles: everyone trades to the core, and no one can game it

  6 people, each owning one room, each ranking all rooms:

    round 1: trading cycle Ann -> Cy -> (back to Ann)
    round 1: trading cycle Di -> Ed -> (back to Di)
    round 1: Fi keeps their own room (self-loop)
    round 2: Bo keeps their own room (self-loop)

  final allocation:
     Ann gets room 2 (Cy's)  (traded up)
      Bo gets room 1 (Bo's)
      Cy gets room 0 (Ann's)  (traded up)
      Di gets room 4 (Ed's)  (traded up)
      Ed gets room 3 (Di's)  (traded up)
      Fi gets room 5 (Fi's)

  individually rational (no one worse off): True
  Pareto efficient: True
  in the core (no coalition can do better): True

  Each person points to the owner of their favourite remaining room; with everyone pointing
  somewhere, a cycle must exist -- its members trade around it and leave. Repeat on the rest.
  The result is the unique core allocation, and lying about preferences can never help you --
  the property that made TTC the basis of kidney-exchange and school-choice mechanisms.
