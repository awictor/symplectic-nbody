Vicsek model -- self-propelled particles flocking, an order-disorder transition
==============================================================================

Each particle aligns to neighbours within radius r, plus angular noise eta. No leader.

N=300, box=7.0, radius r=1.0, density rho=6.12.

Order parameter phi vs noise eta (phi=1 aligned flock, phi=0 disorder):
   eta     phi        regime
   ----------------------------------
   0.50    0.988      flock
   1.50    0.890      flock
   2.50    0.709      flock
   3.50    0.452      critical
   4.50    0.122      disorder
   5.50    0.069      disorder
   6.28    0.051      disorder

Below the critical noise the flock spontaneously aligns; above it, motion is random.
A symmetry-breaking phase transition -- like a magnet, but in a driven, moving system.

Critical noise rises with density (more neighbours to align with):
   N     density   phi at eta=3.0
   ----------------------------------
     80   1.63      0.399
    160   3.27      0.535
    320   6.53      0.626
    640   13.06      0.652
