Quantum circuit simulator: Grover's search by amplitude amplification

  Bell pair (|00> + |11>)/sqrt(2):
    P(00)=0.500  P(01)=0.000  P(10)=0.000  P(11)=0.500
    -> perfectly correlated: only 00 or 11, never 01 or 10 (entanglement)

  Grover search: N = 2^6 = 64 items, marked item = 42
  classical search needs ~32 queries; Grover needs ~6 ((pi/4)sqrt(N))
