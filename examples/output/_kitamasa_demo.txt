Kitamasa: the N-th linear-recurrence term without unrolling

  sequence     char poly                     term 30

  exact small Fibonacci (verified against O(n) unrolling):
    F(10) = 55
    F(50) = 12586269025
    F(100) = 354224848179261915075

  enormous terms modulo p = 1000000007 (impossible by unrolling):
    F(1000000) mod p = 918091266
    F(1000000000000) mod p = 730695249
    F(1000000000000000000) mod p = 209783453

  Kitamasa computes x^n mod c(x) by square-and-multiply (each reduction O(k^2)), then dots
  the resulting coefficient vector with the initial terms. So the N-th term costs only
  O(k^2 log n) -- for n = 10^18 that is a few dozen polynomial multiplies, versus a
  quintillion additions to unroll. It beats even the O(k^3 log n) companion-matrix power.
