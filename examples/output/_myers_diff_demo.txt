  Myers models the diff as a shortest path in an edit graph: diagonal moves (matches) are
  free, right/down moves (delete/insert) cost 1. A BFS over the number of edits D finds the
  corner in O((N+M)*D) -- tiny when the files are similar, which is why git is fast.
