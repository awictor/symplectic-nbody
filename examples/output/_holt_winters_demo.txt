Exponential smoothing: level, trend, and season from a decaying weighted average

  Monthly series (60 months): trend +1.2/month + 12-month seasonal cycle + noise.

            method   12-month forecast RMSE vs truth
        SES (flat)                            19.831
      Holt (trend)                            16.343
      Holt-Winters                             3.270

  SES forecasts a flat line (ignores trend and season); Holt adds the slope but misses
  the seasonal swing; Holt-Winters reinstates both and forecasts the year ahead accurately.
