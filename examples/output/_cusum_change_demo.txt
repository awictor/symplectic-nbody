CUSUM change detection -- catch a small sustained mean shift
============================================================

Stream of 240 values, mean shifts by 1.0 sigma at t=120.
A single value rarely leaves +/-3 sigma, so a Shewhart chart would miss it.

CUSUM alarm at t = 137 (side: high)
  detection delay = 17 steps after the true change
  decision interval H = 5.0

Shewhart 3-sigma rule: only 3 point(s) flagged before CUSUM alarmed.

Detection delay vs shift size (sigma):
   shift   delay (steps)
   ------------------------
     0.5   36
     1.0   12
     1.5   6
     2.0   3
     3.0   1

Bigger shifts trip the accumulating sum faster -- the sequential-detection tradeoff.
