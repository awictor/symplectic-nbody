Wiener filtering: minimum-MSE denoising
==================================================
optimal linear filter: H(f) = S(f) / (S(f) + N(f)), a per-bin trust weight

signal: two tones (5 & 12 cyc), additive noise amplitude 0.7
  SNR noisy:       1.21 dB
  SNR recovered:  19.20 dB  (+18.0 dB)
  MSE noisy:     0.5148
  MSE recovered: 0.0082

the gain passes the signal bins (near 1) and crushes the noise-only bins (near 0):
  gain at bin 5 (tone):  0.985
  gain at bin 12 (tone): 0.959
  gain at bin 40 (noise):0.000

Wiener's filter is optimal among ALL linear time-invariant filters for stationary
signals -- the ancestor of spectral subtraction, deconvolution, and image restoration.
