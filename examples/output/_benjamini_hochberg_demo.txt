Multiple-testing corrections -- FDR (Benjamini-Hochberg) vs FWER
================================================================

100 hypotheses: 15 true effects + 85 nulls, alpha=0.05.

   method        rejected   true+   false+   FDR
   ------------------------------------------------
   uncorrected           18        15       3    0.167
   Bonferroni             3         3       0    0.000
   Holm                   3         3       0    0.000
   Benjamini-Hochberg    16        15       1    0.062

Uncorrected: ~5% of 85 nulls slip through as false positives.
Bonferroni/Holm: control ANY false positive -> conservative, miss real effects.
Benjamini-Hochberg: control the FRACTION of false discoveries -> recovers more signals.

BH cutoff: reject the 16 smallest p-values (largest p rejected = 0.0044).
Bonferroni cutoff: 0.00050 (alpha/m).
