Karp's minimum mean cycle: the loop of least average edge weight, in O(V*E)

    D -> E : -4.0

  Minimum mean cycle: D -> E -> C -> D
  Mean weight: -0.6667  (verified: -0.6667)
  Negative cycle detected (minimum mean < 0).

  Karp's theorem: lambda* = min_v max_k (d_n(v) - d_k(v)) / (n - k), where d_k(v)
  is the least weight of an exactly-k-edge walk from a source. One O(V*E) DP sweep,
  no cycle enumeration. The min mean cycle is the negative-cycle certificate, the
  optimal steady-state of a cyclic schedule, and the pivot in min-cost-flow cancelling.
