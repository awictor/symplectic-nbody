ESPRIT -- subspace frequency estimation beyond the FFT limit
==============================================================

Sampling 100 Hz, 100 samples -> FFT bin spacing = 1.00 Hz.

Two tones at 20.00 and 20.30 Hz (0.3 of a bin apart):
  FFT periodogram: 1 peak (they merge into one blob)
  ESPRIT resolves: [20.0, 20.3] Hz

Noise robustness (2 tones at 8 and 19 Hz, additive Gaussian noise):
   SNR noise-amp    ESPRIT max-err   Prony max-err
   ----------------------------------------------
        0.00      0.0000         0.0000
        0.02      0.0011         0.1052
        0.05      0.0030         0.4618
        0.10      0.0064         0.9119
        0.20      0.0173         8.0000

  ESPRIT projects out the noise subspace before root-finding;
  Prony's linear-prediction step has no such defense.
