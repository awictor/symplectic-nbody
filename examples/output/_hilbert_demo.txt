Hilbert transform: envelope and instantaneous frequency from the analytic signal

  Amplitude modulation: a 25 Hz carrier under a 2 Hz envelope.
    recovered envelope matches the true modulating amplitude to 0.0000

  Linear chirp: instantaneous frequency should rise from 5 Hz to ~42 Hz (below Nyquist).
     time (s)   true freq   measured
          0.3       12.50      12.64
          0.8       25.00      25.54
          1.2       35.00      35.53
          1.6       45.00      45.34

  The analytic signal z = x + i H[x] = A e^{i phi} hands you both: the envelope A(t)
  and the phase phi(t) whose derivative is the instantaneous frequency. AM and FM
  demodulation, vibration diagnostics, and empirical mode decomposition all build on it.
