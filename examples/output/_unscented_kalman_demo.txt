Unscented Kalman filter: Cartesian state from nonlinear range/bearing sensing

  tracked 80 steps of a projectile under gravity
    RMS position error -- raw measurements: 4.485 m
    RMS position error -- UKF estimate:     1.032 m   (4.3x better)

    final true position   (240.00, 10.32)
    final UKF estimate    (239.92, 9.76)

  The measurement model (range, bearing) is nonlinear, so a linear Kalman filter cannot be
  applied directly. The UKF pushes a handful of sigma points through the true nonlinear map
  and recovers the mean and covariance from the transformed cloud -- accurate to second
  order, and needing no Jacobians at all.
