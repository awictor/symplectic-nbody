Belief propagation (sum-product): exact marginals on a tree, by message passing

  A chain of 5 binary spins; neighbours prefer to agree (factor [[3,1],[1,3]]),
  and s0 is biased toward state 1 (unary [1,4]).

  Factor graph is a tree: True

   variable   P(state=1) BP     brute
         s0          0.8000    0.8000
         s1          0.6500    0.6500
         s2          0.5750    0.5750
         s3          0.5375    0.5375
         s4          0.5187    0.5188

  The bias at s0 propagates down the chain, decaying with distance -- BP captures it
  exactly, matching brute enumeration, in time linear in the graph.

  Partition function Z = 1280.000
  MAP (most probable) configuration: [1, 1, 1, 1, 1]
