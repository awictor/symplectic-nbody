t-digest: p50/p90/p99/p999 from one pass, no storing the stream

  streamed 500,000 latency samples through a digest of 64 centroids
    memory: 64 centroids vs 500,000 raw values (~7,812x smaller)

  quantile      t-digest      exact      rank error
    p50.00        18.919      18.879    0.00094
    p90.00        51.198      51.144    0.00024
    p99.00        97.711      97.051    0.00032
    p99.90       150.087     143.090    0.00028
    p99.99       236.836     195.029    0.00008

  distributed roll-up: 8 per-shard digests merged into one
    merged p99 = 97.944  vs single-digest p99 = 97.711
    vs exact p99 = 97.051

  The scale function k(q) = (delta/2pi) arcsin(2q-1) is compressed at the tails, so
  centroids there hold almost no weight (fine resolution) while a single centroid near the
  median absorbs a big slice (coarse, but who cares). Merges are associative -> distributed.
