Lloyd-Max quantizer -- minimum-distortion levels for a known source
====================================================================

Gaussian source N(0,1). Lloyd-Max places levels where the mass is;
a uniform quantizer spaces them evenly and wastes levels in the tails.

SNR vs number of levels (Lloyd-Max minus uniform):
   levels   uniform SNR   Lloyd-Max SNR   gain
   ----------------------------------------------
      2        -5.13           4.40     +9.53 dB
      4         2.61           9.30     +6.69 dB
      8         8.85          14.62     +5.76 dB
     16        14.87          20.22     +5.34 dB
     32        20.89          24.82     +3.92 dB

Lloyd-Max levels (N=8), note the crowding near 0:
  -2.15  -1.34  -0.76  -0.25  +0.25  +0.76  +1.34  +2.15
  gaps: 0.81  0.59  0.51  0.49  0.51  0.59  0.81
  (smallest gap 0.49 near center, largest 0.81 in the tails)

Converged in 73 iterations, distortion 0.0345.
