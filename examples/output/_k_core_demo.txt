  Smallest-last peeling removes a minimum-degree vertex at a time; the degree it has when
  removed is its coreness. The maximum coreness is the degeneracy, and the resulting order
  bounds clique- and coloring-algorithm runtimes. High-coreness nodes are the robust,
  deeply-embedded core of the network -- the influential centre in social-network analysis.
