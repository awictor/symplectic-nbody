  complexity comparison (sub-multiplications per level, and the resulting exponent):
    method        mults  splits  exponent O(n^e)
    schoolbook        4       2           2.0000
    Karatsuba         3       2           1.5850
    Toom-3            5       3           1.4650

  Karatsuba: split each number in 2, and the middle cross-term x1*y0 + x0*y1 comes from
  ONE product (x1+x0)(y1+y0) minus the two you already have -- 3 mults, not 4. Toom-3 splits
  in 3 and interpolates a degree-4 product from 5 evaluations, not 9. Each cut of the
  exponent matters enormously for the thousand-digit numbers inside bignum libraries.
