Discrete wavelet transform: seeing a signal at every scale at once

  128-point signal (smooth wave + transient burst near index 80), db4 wavelet:
    7 detail levels, sizes [64, 32, 16, 8, 4, 2, 1], approx size 1
    finest-detail peak at coefficient 41 -> signal index ~82 (the burst is localised in time, unlike a Fourier spectrum)

    top  8 of 128 coefficients hold 88.4% of the energy
    top 16 of 128 coefficients hold 98.9% of the energy
    top 32 of 128 coefficients hold 100.0% of the energy

  denoising by soft-thresholding detail coefficients:
    RMS error vs clean -- noisy 0.1010, denoised 0.0731 (1.4x cleaner)

  A filter bank splits the signal into a coarse approximation and fine detail, then recurses
  on the approximation -- a pyramid that captures both what frequencies are present and WHERE.
  db4's two vanishing moments make smooth stretches nearly vanish, so energy piles into a few
  coefficients: the basis of wavelet compression and denoising.
