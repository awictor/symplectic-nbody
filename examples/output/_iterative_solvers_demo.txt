Stationary iterative solvers: Jacobi, Gauss-Seidel, SOR on the 2-D Poisson equation

  -Laplacian(u) = 2 pi^2 sin(pi x) sin(pi y) on a 14x14 interior grid.
  Manufactured exact solution u = sin(pi x) sin(pi y).

          method   iterations   max error
          jacobi          815    3.62e-03
    gauss_seidel          409    3.62e-03
             sor           55    3.62e-03

  Optimal SOR omega = 1.6558
  SOR is 14.8x fewer iterations than Jacobi -- O(N) vs O(N^2) scaling.
  All three converge to the same solution; only the speed differs.
