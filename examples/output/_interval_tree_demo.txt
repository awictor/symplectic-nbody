Interval tree: every interval overlapping a point or range, without scanning all

  8 meetings loaded into the tree

  stabbing query -- who is busy at a given hour?
    at 9:00 -> ['design', 'standup']
    at 13:00 -> ['1:1', 'deep-work', 'lunch-review']
    at 15:00 -> ['deep-work', 'sync']
    at 19:00 -> free

  range query -- what clashes with a proposed 14:00-16:00 slot?
    clashes: ['1:1', 'deep-work', 'interview', 'sync']

  A centered interval tree stores intervals containing the median endpoint at each node,
  kept in two sorted lists (by start, by end). A stabbing query walks one list until it
  passes the point and recurses into only the relevant subtree -- O(log n + hits), never a
  full scan, which is how genome browsers and calendars stay fast on millions of intervals.
