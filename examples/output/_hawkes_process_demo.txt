Hawkes process -- self-exciting events (earthquakes, spikes, orders)
====================================================================

Baseline mu=1.0, decay beta=2.0. Self-excitation amplifies the rate by 1/(1-n).

Branching ratio n = alpha/beta controls clustering and the stationary rate mu/(1-n):
   alpha   n=a/b    theoretical rate   empirical rate
   --------------------------------------------------
     0.0   0.000       1.000          0.991
     0.5   0.250       1.333          1.313
     1.0   0.500       2.000          1.976
     1.5   0.750       4.000          3.859
     1.8   0.900      10.000          9.366

n=0 is plain Poisson; n->1 the rate blows up as excitation feeds on itself.

Clustering: coefficient of variation^2 of inter-event gaps (Poisson = 1):
  Hawkes  (n=0.4): CV^2 = 1.68  (bursty -- gaps very uneven)
  Poisson (same rate): CV^2 = 0.96  (memoryless)
