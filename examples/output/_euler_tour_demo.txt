Euler tour: flattening a tree so subtrees become array ranges
==============================================================
8-node tree, rooted at 0

 node  tin  tout  depth   subtree range  size
    0    0     7      0          [0, 7]     8
    1    1     4      1          [1, 4]     4
    2    5     7      1          [5, 7]     3
    3    2     2      2          [2, 2]     1
    4    3     4      2          [3, 4]     2
    5    6     6      2          [6, 6]     1
    6    7     7      2          [7, 7]     1
    7    4     4      3          [4, 4]     1

tour order (nodes by entry time): 0 1 3 4 7 2 5 6

queries that become O(1) or O(log n) on the flattened array:
  is 1 an ancestor of 7?  True  (tin/tout containment)
  is 2 an ancestor of 7?  False
  subtree of 1 = [1, 3, 4, 7]
  subtree_sum(1) with values 1..8 = 19

Every subtree is a contiguous slice of tour time, so subtree sums/updates
reduce to range operations on an array (pair with a Fenwick tree for O(log n)).
