Cepstrum: the spectrum of a log-spectrum
==================================================
cepstrum(x) = IDFT( log|DFT(x)| ); its axis is 'quefrency' (time-like)
a convolution (envelope * excitation) becomes a SUM after the log, so the
two separate cleanly in the cepstral domain.

pitch detection: voiced signal, f0 = 125 Hz, fs = 8000
  true pitch period: 64.0 samples (125 Hz)
  cepstral peak at quefrency 64 samples -> 125.0 Hz

echo detection: signal + a copy delayed by 35 samples
  cepstral peak at quefrency 35 samples (true delay 35)

Cepstral pitch tracking finds the fundamental even when it is weak or missing,
because it keys on the harmonic SPACING, not the fundamental's own energy.
