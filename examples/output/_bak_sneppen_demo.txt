Bak-Sneppen model -- evolution self-organizes to the edge of chaos
==================================================================

N species on a ring; each step the LEAST-fit species and its 2 neighbours get
fresh random fitness. No tuning -- the system finds its own critical state.

Ring of 200 species, 400k updates:
  self-organized threshold (gap): 0.665  (1-D theory f_c ~ 0.667)
  final mean fitness: 0.809
  fraction above f_c: 0.88

The gap (running max of the minimum fitness) climbs to f_c and plateaus:
   updates      gap so far
   --------------------------
        10      0.026
       100      0.298
      1000      0.533
     10000      0.629
    100000      0.665
    400000      0.665
