Smith Normal Form: integer diagonalization D = U A V, and the group it encodes

  A =
         2    4    4
        -6    6   12
        10   -4  -16

  D = U A V (Smith Normal Form) =
         2    0    0
         0    6    0
         0    0   12

  U A V = D verified: True
  U unimodular: True,  V unimodular: True

  invariant factors: [2, 6, 12]  (each divides the next: True)

  cokernel Z^3 / A Z^3 = Z/2 x Z/6 x Z/12
  (this is exactly how a homology group H = ker/im is computed: SNF of the
  boundary matrix gives the torsion coefficients and the free rank = Betti number.)
