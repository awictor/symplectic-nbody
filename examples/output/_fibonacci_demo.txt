Fibonacci by fast doubling: O(log n), so million-digit terms are instant

  F_10 = 55
  F_100 = 35422484817926191507... (21 digits)
  F_1000 = 43466557686937456435... (209 digits)
  F_1000000 has ~208988 digits (computed via ~20 big-integer multiplies)

  Cassini's identity F_(n-1)F_(n+1) - F_n^2 = (-1)^n:
    n=5: -1
    n=6: +1
    n=7: -1
    n=8: +1

  GCD property gcd(F_m, F_n) = F_gcd(m,n):
    gcd(F_12, F_18) = 8 = F_6 = 8
    gcd(F_15, F_25) = 5 = F_5 = 5
    gcd(F_14, F_21) = 13 = F_7 = 13

  Pisano period pi(m) (period of F_n mod m) lets F_n mod m work for huge n:
    pi(2) = 3
    pi(3) = 8
    pi(10) = 60
    pi(100) = 300
    pi(1000) = 1500
  F_(10^100) mod 1000 = 875  (via the Pisano cycle)

  Zeckendorf representations (unique non-consecutive Fibonacci sums):
    17 = 13 + 3 + 1
    100 = 89 + 8 + 3
    1000 = 987 + 13
