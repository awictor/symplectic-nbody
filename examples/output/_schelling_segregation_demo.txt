Schelling segregation -- mild preference, total segregation (emergence)
======================================================================

40x40 grid, 10% empty, tolerance tau=0.33 (each agent wants only 1/3 same-type).
  initial similarity: 0.502
  final similarity:   0.756  (segregation, from a mild wish!)
  converged in 11 rounds, 100% happy.

Nobody wanted segregation -- everyone was content with a 1/3 minority -- yet blocks form.

Equilibrium segregation vs tolerance threshold tau:

   ----------------------------------------
   0.00    0.493              100%

   0.60    0.974              99%
   0.75    0.842              77%

Higher demands -> more segregation; but even the tiny tau=0.33 lands at ~0.75 similarity.
