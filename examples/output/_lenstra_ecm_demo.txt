Lenstra's elliptic-curve method (ECM)
==================================================
factor N by running k*P on a random curve mod N until an inversion fails --
the failed gcd hands you a factor. Fresh curve = fresh dice, so no single
unlucky factorization defeats it (unlike Pollard p-1).

                      case                     N   factorization
           small semiprime                  1073   29 * 37
          Project Euler #3          600851475143   71 * 839 * 1471 * 6857
  10-digit-prime semiprime   1000000016000000063   1000000007 * 1000000009
             mixed factors                 36360   2 * 2 * 2 * 3 * 3 * 5 * 101
               prime power                 16807   7 * 7 * 7 * 7 * 7
                   a prime            9999999967   9999999967

Each factorization is verified: the primes multiply back to N and each passes
the Baillie-PSW primality test. ECM is the method of choice for pulling out
medium (up to ~40-digit) factors before a heavier sieve finishes the job.
