Sum over subsets (SOS DP): cumulative sums on the boolean hypercube in O(n 2^n)

  Universe of 3 elements, f(S) = |S|:

         S  f(S)     F(S)=sum_{T<=S} f(T)
        {}     0                        0
       {0}     1                        1
       {1}     1                        1
     {0,1}     2                        4
       {2}     1                        1
     {0,2}     2                        4
     {1,2}     2                        4
   {0,1,2}     3                       12

  Moebius inverse recovers f exactly: True

  OR-convolution of the singleton indicator with itself (ordered pairs of singletons
  whose UNION is S):
    {0}: 1
    {1}: 1
    {0,1}: 2
    {2}: 1
    {0,2}: 2
    {1,2}: 2

  Subset-sum convolution (DISJOINT pairs of singletons; union is S, no shared element):
    {0,1}: 2
    {0,2}: 2
    {1,2}: 2

  Note the difference: OR-conv counts {0}|{0}={0}; subset-sum conv forbids reusing 0,
  so only 2-element sets get a count (2, the two orderings). Disjointness for free via
  the ranked-by-popcount zeta trick.
