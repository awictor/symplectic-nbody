Ehrenfest urn -- irreversible diffusion from reversible microscopic moves
==========================================================================

50 balls, all starting in urn A. Each step moves one random ball to the other urn.

A single trajectory (count in urn A) relaxes from N toward N/2 then fluctuates:
   step     count A
   ----------------------
      0     50
     20     34
     50     26
    100     24
    200     28
    400     28
  equilibrium is N/2 = 25; fluctuations of order sqrt(N) ~ 7.1 persist.

Stationary distribution is Binomial(N, 1/2) -- each ball independently in either urn:
  peak at k=25 (=N/2), pi_peak=0.1123, pi at all-in-one=8.88e-16
  detailed balance residual: 6.9e-18 (reversible chain)

Entropy of the ensemble distribution rises as the gas spreads (Boltzmann's H-theorem):
   step     entropy (bits)
   ----------------------------
      0     0.000
      5     0.757
     15     2.083
     40     2.731
    100     2.867

Poincare recurrence IS satisfied -- but the mean return time to all-in-one is 2^N:
   N= 10: mean recurrence = 2^10 = 1.024e+03 steps
   N= 30: mean recurrence = 2^30 = 1.074e+09 steps
   N= 50: mean recurrence = 2^50 = 1.126e+15 steps
   N=100: mean recurrence = 2^100 = 1.268e+30 steps
  (for N=100 that dwarfs the age of the universe -- why gases never un-mix).
