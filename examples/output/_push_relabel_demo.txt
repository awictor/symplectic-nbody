  minimum cut: source side = [0, 1, 2, 4]
    cut capacity = 23  (equals max flow 23: max-flow min-cut theorem)

  Push-relabel keeps a PREFLOW where nodes hold excess, and a HEIGHT per node. It PUSHES
  excess to a lower neighbour, or RELABELS (lifts) a stuck node until it can push. Excess
  that cannot reach the sink is lifted above the source and drains back; when only the sink
  holds excess, the preflow has become a maximum flow.
