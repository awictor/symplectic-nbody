MFCC: mel-frequency cepstral coefficients, the front end of speech recognition

  input: 0.5s chirp sweeping 300 Hz -> 3000 Hz at 8000 Hz sample rate
  output: 49 frames x 13 coefficients

  mel scale warps frequency (fine low, coarse high):
          Hz       mel
         100     150.5
         500     607.4
        1000    1000.0
        2000    1521.4
        4000    2146.1

  MFCC[0] (log-energy) range over the sweep: -50.20 .. -36.79
  MFCC[2] (spectral shape) range:            -11.95 .. 6.59

  The heatmap's coefficient pattern shifts as the pitch rises -- exactly the
  compact, decorrelated representation a recognizer classifies on.
