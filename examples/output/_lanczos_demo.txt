  matrix-free: Laplacian of a 500-node ring graph (never stored as a matrix):
    largest eigenvalue:  3.99973  (analytic max 4 -- outermost, converges fast)
    smallest eigenvalue: 1.62e-04  (analytic 0, the constant null vector)
    (the tightly clustered small eigenvalues need more iterations than the extremes)

  The Krylov vectors v, Av, A^2 v, ... quickly span the dominant eigen-directions; a
  three-term recurrence orthogonalises them into a tiny tridiagonal matrix whose eigenvalues
  (Ritz values) approximate the extremes. An n-million eigenproblem becomes an m-by-m one.
