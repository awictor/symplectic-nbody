Orthogonal matching pursuit: sparse recovery from compressed measurements

  Signal length n = 60, sparsity k = 4 (only 4 nonzero coefficients).

   measurements m   recovered support?   max coeff error
                8                   no          3.00e+00
               15                   no          2.50e+00
               20                  YES          8.88e-16
               25                  YES          4.44e-16

  Around m ~ 20 random measurements (a few times k) OMP nails all 60 coefficients
  exactly, though the system is wildly underdetermined -- the sparsity is what makes it
  solvable. This is the principle behind MRI acceleration and single-pixel cameras.
