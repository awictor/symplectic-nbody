TASEP -- driven lattice gas with an exactly-solved nonequilibrium phase diagram
==============================================================================

Particles hop right at rate 1; injected left at rate alpha, ejected right at rate beta.

Steady-state density and current vs the exact formulas:
   alpha  beta   phase            rho (sim/exact)   J (sim/exact)
   --------------------------------------------------------------
   0.25  0.80   low-density      0.250/0.250      0.187/0.188
   0.80  0.25   high-density     0.748/0.750      0.187/0.188
   0.80  0.80   maximal-current  0.486/0.500      0.253/0.250
   0.40  0.40   low-density      0.474/0.400      0.239/0.240

Three phases: low-density (rho=alpha), high-density (rho=1-beta), maximal-current (rho=1/2).
The maximal current J=1/4 is a hard ceiling on single-file transport.
