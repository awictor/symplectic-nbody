Domain warping: feeding noise into its own coordinates
========================================================
warp(x) = fbm(x + amplitude * fbm(x + offset)); each level folds the plane again

total variation of a 1-D slice grows with each warp level (more swirl):
  warp levels   total variation
            0            24.016
            1            61.259
            2           133.542

warp displacement vectors (level 1) at sample points:
  (1.0, 1.0) -> displaced by (+0.000, -0.125)
  (3.0, 2.0) -> displaced by (+0.000, -0.072)
  (5.5, 4.5) -> displaced by (-0.110, +0.103)

The inner noise drags the sampling point sideways, turning smooth ridges into
the whorls and filaments of marble, wood grain, smoke, and magma.
