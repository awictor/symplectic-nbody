Poisson-disk sampling: random-looking points that never crowd (blue noise)

  Bridson Poisson-disk: 301 points, min distance 14.02 (radius 14.0)
    insertable gap fraction 0.0000 (near-maximal packing)

  Uniform random (301 points): min distance 0.54
    -> pairs land far closer than 14.0; the pattern clumps and leaves holes

  Bridson keeps a background grid of cells r/sqrt(2) across, so each holds at most one
  sample and a candidate is tested against only its constant-size neighbourhood -- O(n)
  overall. Every accepted point sits in the annulus [r, 2r) of an active sample, packing
  tightly while never violating the minimum distance.
