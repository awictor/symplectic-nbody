Hopfield network -- content-addressable memory by energy descent
==========================================================================

Stored 3 letter patterns in an N=25 neuron net (5x5 bitmaps).
Each stored pattern is a fixed point: True. Energies:
   'T'  E =  -11.200
   'L'  E =  -11.200
   'X'  E =  -11.040

Show it a corrupted 'T' (3 of 25 pixels flipped) and let it settle:

    corrupted cue     ->     recalled      (stored 'T')
      ##.##          #####         #####
      ..#..          ..#..         ..#..
      ..#..          ..#..         ..#..
      ..##.          ..#..         ..#..
      #.#..          ..#..         ..#..

  overlap with stored 'T' = +1.000  (1.0 = perfect recall).

Capacity: recall accuracy vs load p/N (N=100, 10 flipped bits per cue):
   load p/N     patterns     mean recall overlap
   ----------------------------------------------
    0.02           2         1.000  ##############################
    0.05           5         1.000  ##############################
    0.10          10         0.994  #############################
    0.14          14         0.964  ############################
    0.20          20         0.914  ###########################
    0.30          30         0.651  ###################

Recall stays near-perfect until the Amit-Gutfreund-Sompolinsky load p/N ~ 0.138, then collapses as spurious minima take over.
