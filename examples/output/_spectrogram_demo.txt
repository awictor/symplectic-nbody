Spectrogram: short-time Fourier transform
==================================================
signal 2048 samples at 256 Hz: chirp 10->70 Hz + steady 40 Hz + late 95 Hz burst
STFT: frame 128, hop 32, Hann window -> 64 frames x 65 bins
bin resolution: 2.00 Hz, time resolution: 125.0 ms

dominant-frequency ridge over time (first / mid / last frames):
  start: 14.0 Hz, middle: 74.0 Hz, end: 128.0 Hz

The uncertainty principle: a shorter frame sharpens timing but blurs frequency;
a longer frame does the reverse. The Hann window suppresses spectral leakage.
