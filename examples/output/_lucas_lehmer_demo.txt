Lucas-Lehmer: the deterministic test behind every record-breaking prime

  M_7 = 2^7 - 1 = 127
  s_0=4, s_(k+1)=s_k^2-2 mod 127:  [4, 14, 67, 42, 111, 0]
  s_(p-2) = s_5 = 0  ->  PRIME

  Mersenne primes M_p for p <= 150:  p in [2, 3, 5, 7, 13, 17, 19, 31, 61, 89, 107, 127]
  (only 12 of the primes up to 150 yield a Mersenne prime -- they are rare)

  digit sizes of the Mersenne primes found:
        p   digits of M_p
        2               1
        3               1
        5               2
        7               3
       13               4
       17               6
       19               6
       31              10
       61              19
       89              27
      107              33

  The test is p-2 squarings mod (2^p - 1) -- and the modular reduction is a cheap
  bit fold, so it scales to exponents in the tens of millions. That is exactly what
  the GIMPS project runs to find primes with over 20 million digits.
