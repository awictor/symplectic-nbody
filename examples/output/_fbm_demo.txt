Fractional Brownian motion: layered noise octaves
====================================================
fBm(x) = sum gain^i * noise(lacunarity^i * x); each octave doubles the
frequency and halves the amplitude -- self-similar detail across scales.

octaves=6, lacunarity=2.0, gain=0.5
max amplitude (geometric sum): 1.9688

total variation of a 1-D slice grows as octaves are added (more detail):
  octaves   total variation
        1            34.101
        2            44.682
        3            52.876
        4            57.572
        6            60.556
        8            60.917

variants: fBm = signed sum (terrain), turbulence = sum|noise| (clouds),
ridged = sum(1-|noise|)^2 (sharp mountain ridges).
