Wilcoxon signed-rank test -- robust paired comparison
======================================================

12 subjects, 'after - before' (a consistent improvement):
  differences: [0.9, 0.7, 1.2, 0.9, 0.7, 1.6, 0.8, 1.5, 0.8, 1.5, 0.7, 1.3]
  W+ = 78.0, W- = 0.0
  Wilcoxon p (greater) = 0.0012 (normal-approx)
  paired t-test p = 0.0000  (agrees here)

One subject's difference is a data-entry error (-50):
  Wilcoxon p (greater) = 0.0187  -- still sees the improvement
  paired t-test  p     = 0.4445  -- outlier destroys it
  The rank test caps the outlier's influence at one rank; the t-test averages it in.

Exact null distribution of W+ for n=12 (2^12 = 4096 sign patterns):
  W+ ranges 0..78, symmetric about 39
  observed W+ = 78 sits in the extreme upper tail
