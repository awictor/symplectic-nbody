Moran process -- finite-population evolution, one birth & death per step
========================================================================

Population N=12. Fixation probability of type-A from i copies, fitness ratio r:
   i    r=0.5 (del)   r=1.0 (neutral)   r=2.0 (adv)
   ------------------------------------------------
    1    0.0002        0.0833            0.5001
    3    0.0017        0.2500            0.8752
    6    0.0154        0.5000            0.9846
    9    0.1248        0.7500            0.9983
   11    0.4999        0.9167            0.9998

Neutral fixation = i/N exactly; advantage bends the curve up, disadvantage down.

A single advantageous mutant (i=1) fixes with prob (1-1/r)/(1-1/r^N):
   r      N=12     N=100    N=1000    limit 1-1/r
   ----------------------------------------------
    1.1   0.1334   0.0909   0.0909    0.0909
    1.5   0.3359   0.3333   0.3333    0.3333
    2.0   0.5001   0.5000   0.5000    0.5000
    3.0   0.6667   0.6667   0.6667    0.6667

Even a strongly-beneficial mutant is far from guaranteed to fix -- drift can lose it early.

Validation (N=12, i0=3, r=1.5): formula 0.709, empirical 0.716 over 5000 runs.
