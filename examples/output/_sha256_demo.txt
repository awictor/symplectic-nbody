  avalanche effect (change one character of the input):
    sha256('avalanche') vs sha256('avalanchf')
    136 of 256 output bits differ (53% -- a total scramble)

  HMAC-SHA256 (keyed message authentication):
    HMAC(key, 'message 1') = 7569d58639ed415bbea927fe723bd4ec...
    HMAC(key, 'message 2') = 85109959d247e659522525ec8be9c213...
    (unforgeable without the key; how APIs sign requests)

  The message is padded to a multiple of 512 bits, split into blocks, and each block is
  folded into a 256-bit state by 64 rounds of bitwise mixing (choice, majority, rotations,
  and constants from the cube roots of primes). Every implementation must agree to the bit,
  which is why the whole internet can share one hash function.
