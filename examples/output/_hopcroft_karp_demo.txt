Hopcroft-Karp: maximum bipartite matching in O(E sqrt(V))

  5 applicants, 4 jobs, 9 qualifications.

  Who qualifies for what:
     Ada: Backend, Data
     Ben: Backend, Frontend
     Cid: Frontend, DevOps
     Dot: Data, DevOps
     Eli: Frontend

  Maximum matching size: 4  (jobs filled without double-booking)
     Ada -> Backend
     Ben -> Frontend
     Cid -> DevOps
     Dot -> Data
    unassigned: Eli

  Koenig minimum vertex cover (size 4 == matching 4):
    Backend (job), Frontend (job), Data (job), DevOps (job)

  Koenig's theorem: in any bipartite graph the largest matching equals the smallest
  set of vertices touching every edge. The cover is the bottleneck -- pick these few
  people/roles and every qualification runs through one of them.
