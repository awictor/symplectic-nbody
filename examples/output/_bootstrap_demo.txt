Bootstrap: confidence intervals by resampling, no formula needed

  sample of 80 draws (true mean 50): observed mean 50.868, median 51.315

  the MEAN (which does have a formula, so we can check ourselves):
    bootstrap SE 0.7444  vs analytic s/sqrt(n) 0.7523
    95% percentile CI: [49.469, 52.325]  (brackets 50: True)
    95% BCa CI:        [49.468, 52.324]
    jackknife bias 0.00e+00 (exactly 0 for the mean), SE 0.7523

  the MEDIAN (no simple closed-form CI -- the bootstrap just works):
    95% percentile CI: [48.829, 52.905]
    95% BCa CI:        [48.685, 52.905]

  the SPREAD (standard deviation), likewise:
    95% BCa CI for the std: [5.684, 8.188]  (true 8)

  The bootstrap treats the sample as a stand-in for the population: resample it with
  replacement thousands of times, recompute the statistic each time, and the spread of
  those replicates is the sampling distribution -- read off the CI from its percentiles.
  BCa additionally corrects for median bias and skewness (via the jackknife) for sharper
  coverage. It works for ANY statistic, however awkward its variance formula would be.
