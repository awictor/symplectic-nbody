==========================================================

T_MRCA approaches 2 (most depth is the last two lineages merging);
total length grows only as 2 H_(n-1) (logarithmically) -- adding samples adds little.

Watterson's estimator of theta = 4 N mu from segregating sites S:
   true theta   n    mean S    theta_hat = S / H_(n-1)
   ------------------------------------------------
        1.0    20     3.50    0.987
        5.0    20    17.69    4.987
       10.0    20    35.38    9.971

S grows with both mutation rate and (log of) sample size; theta_hat inverts it.
