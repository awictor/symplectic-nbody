Affine-gap alignment (Gotoh): one long gap beats many short ones

  Aligning:
    a = ACGTACGTACGT
    b = ACGTACAAAGGGTACGT

  Linear gap model (each gap char costs the same):
    ACGTAC-----GTACGT
    ACGTACAAAGGGTACGT   score 19
    gap runs: 1

  Affine gap model (open -4, extend -1: one big gap is cheap):
    ACGTAC-----GTACGT
    ACGTACAAAGGGTACGT   score 15.0
    gap runs: 1

  With affine scoring the insertion aligns as ONE contiguous gap, matching the single
  biological indel event -- linear scoring has no reason to prefer that over scattered gaps.

  Local affine alignment of an embedded motif:
    GATTACA
    GATTACA   score 14.0
