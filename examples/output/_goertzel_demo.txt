Goertzel algorithm: single-frequency detection without the full FFT

  Tone detection: a 1000 Hz sinusoid buried in strong noise.
   freq (Hz)   Goertzel power
         500            208.5  
         800            493.2  
        1000         144558.1  ##############################
        1200             50.2  
        1500            168.4  
        2000            388.9  
    -> strongest at 1000 Hz (the hidden tone), one O(n) recurrence per probe.

  DTMF touch-tone decoding: dial a number, decode from the audio.
    dialled:  1-800-2468
    decoded:  1-800-2468   (match: True)

  Each key is a sum of a row and a column tone; two Goertzel detectors per band find
  the strongest, and the row x column intersection names the key -- how real phones do it.
