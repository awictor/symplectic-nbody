    (small R = cheap control = aggressive gain, fast settle; large R = gentle)

  naturally unstable system (both open-loop modes > 1):
    open-loop spectral radius:  1.500  (unstable)
    closed-loop spectral radius: 0.443  (stable under LQR)

  The optimal control u = -K x uses a constant gain K built from the Riccati solution P.
  It provably minimises the total quadratic cost -- state error plus control effort -- over
  all time, which is why LQR is the inner loop of drones, rockets, and robots everywhere.
