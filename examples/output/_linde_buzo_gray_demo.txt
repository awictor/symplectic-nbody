Linde-Buzo-Gray -- vector-quantization codebook by splitting + refining
========================================================================

400 points on a spiral, codebook grown 1 -> 2 -> 4 -> 8 -> 16.

Distortion as the codebook doubles:
   size   distortion
   ----------------------
      2   0.40608
      4   0.19058
      8   0.05424
     16   0.01865

Rate-distortion (bits per vector = log2 size):
   bits   size   distortion
   ------------------------------
      1      2   0.40608
      2      4   0.19058
      3      8   0.05424
      4     16   0.01865
      5     32   0.00929

Each extra bit halves the codebook spacing -> ~4x lower distortion (1/N per dim).
