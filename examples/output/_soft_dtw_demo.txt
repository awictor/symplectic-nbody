Soft-DTW: differentiable dynamic time warping
====================================================
series lengths: 20 and 24 (y has a time-stretched plateau)

hard DTW cost: 0.00000

soft-DTW converges to hard DTW as the temperature gamma -> 0:
     gamma      soft-DTW   |soft - hard|
       1.0     -31.45446        3.15e+01
       0.3      -8.39049        8.39e+00
       0.1      -2.20686        2.21e+00
      0.03      -0.36215        3.62e-01
      0.01      -0.05901        5.90e-02
     0.001      -0.00072        7.17e-04

Large gamma averages over ALL alignment paths (smooth, differentiable);
small gamma concentrates on the single optimal path (classic DTW).
The soft alignment matrix is the gradient of soft-DTW w.r.t. the cost matrix,
which is what lets soft-DTW be used as a loss and to average series under warping.
