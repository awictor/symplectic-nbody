Slice sampling: self-tuning MCMC for a bimodal density
========================================================
target: 0.6*N(-2, 0.6) + 0.4*N(2.5, 1.0)
drew 20000 samples, no proposal-width tuning (stepping-out + shrinkage)

    quantity   empirical    analytic
        mean      -0.170      -0.200
    variance       5.549       5.476
      P(x<0)       0.596       0.600

Both modes are explored with the right relative mass, with no accept/reject tuning:
the slice width adapts to the local shape of the distribution.
