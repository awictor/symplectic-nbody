Pratt certificate: a short, checkable proof of primality
==========================================================
Lucas test: n is prime iff some witness a has a^(n-1)=1 and
a^((n-1)/q)!=1 for every prime q | n-1 -- and each such q is itself certified.

certificate for 997 (10 nodes):
  997: witness a=7, 997-1 factors into [2, 3, 83]
    2  (base case)
    3: witness a=2, 3-1 factors into [2]
      2  (base case)
    83: witness a=2, 83-1 factors into [2, 41]
      2  (base case)
      41: witness a=6, 41-1 factors into [2, 5]
        2  (base case)
        5: witness a=2, 5-1 factors into [2]
          2  (base case)

verifies? True

certificate sizes (nodes) grow polynomially in the number of digits:
     prime   nodes  witness
         7       4        3
       101       4        2
       997      10        7
      7919      14        7
    104729      16       12
   1299709      20        6

This is why PRIMES is in NP: the certificate is short and anyone can re-check it
with a handful of modular exponentiations -- no need to redo the primality search.
