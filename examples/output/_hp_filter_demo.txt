Hodrick-Prescott filter: split a time series into a smooth trend and a cycle

  Synthetic series (80 points): quadratic trend + 20-period cycle + noise.

      lambda   trend roughness   cycle std
          10            5.2327       0.820
         100            1.3510       1.593
        1600            0.0504       2.614  <- classic quarterly lambda
       10000            0.0203       2.846
      100000            0.0048       3.937

  Small lambda tracks every wiggle (trend ~ data, tiny cycle); large lambda gives an
  almost-straight trend and pushes the cycle up. Lambda=1600 is the textbook quarterly
  choice. The trend minimizes fidelity + lambda * (second-difference)^2 exactly.
