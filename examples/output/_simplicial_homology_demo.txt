Simplicial homology: counting holes via boundary matrices + Smith Normal Form

    space             V   E   F   Betti (b0,b1,b2)  torsion    chi
    circle S^1        3   3   0   (1, 1)            -            0
    disk D^2          3   3   1   (1, 0, 0)         -            1
    sphere S^2        4   6   4   (1, 0, 1)         -            2
    figure-eight      5   6   0   (1, 2)            -           -1
    torus T^2         7  21  14   (1, 2, 1)         -            0
    proj plane RP^2   6  15  10   (1, 0, 0)         H1:Z/2       1

  b_0 = connected components, b_1 = independent loops, b_2 = enclosed voids.
  The torus has b_1 = 2 (the two independent loops around it); RP^2 has a Z/2
  torsion class -- a loop that is not null-homotopic but whose DOUBLE bounds.
  Every space passes d^2 = 0 and the Euler-Poincare identity chi = sum (-1)^k b_k.
