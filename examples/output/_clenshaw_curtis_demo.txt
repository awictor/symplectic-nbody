Clenshaw-Curtis quadrature: spectral accuracy from Chebyshev-clustered nodes

  integrand: e^-x / (2 + cos 3x) on [0.0, 2.0]
  reference integral (n=512): 0.47679845656238

        n   Clenshaw-Curtis err     trapezoid err
        4              3.10e-02          8.89e-03
        8              9.50e-04          1.43e-03
       16              8.02e-07          3.57e-04
       32              1.56e-11          8.94e-05
       64              0.00e+00          2.23e-05

  Clenshaw-Curtis error falls off a cliff (spectral) while trapezoid decays like
  O(1/n^2). At n=32 Clenshaw-Curtis is already near machine precision.
  Its nodes cluster toward the endpoints (Chebyshev extrema), killing edge error,
  and they nest -- doubling n reuses every previous sample.
