  Golden section keeps a bracket guaranteed to hold the minimum and shrinks it by ~0.618
  each step -- slow but foolproof on any unimodal curve. Brent tries a fast parabolic jump
  and falls back to golden section when the parabola misbehaves, getting superlinear speed
  with the same guarantee -- the default 1D minimizer inside every optimizer's line search.
