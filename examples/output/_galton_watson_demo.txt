Galton-Watson branching process -- extinction or explosion?
============================================================

Extinction probability q = smallest fixed point of s = G(s):
   offspring dist         mean m   regime         q (extinction)
   --------------------------------------------------------------
   p0=.5 p1=.3 p2=.2        0.70   subcritical    1.0000
   p0=.25 p1=.5 p2=.25      1.00   critical       1.0000
   p0=.2 p1=.3 p2=.5        1.30   supercritical  0.4000
   p0=.3 p2=.7 (fission)    1.40   supercritical  0.4286
   p0=.1 p1=.2 p2=.7        1.60   supercritical  0.1429

Subcritical & critical (m<=1): certain extinction. Supercritical (m>1): q<1, chance of growth.

Supercritical p0=.2 p1=.3 p2=.5 (m=1.3): theoretical q=0.400, empirical extinction=0.401 over 4000 lineages.
