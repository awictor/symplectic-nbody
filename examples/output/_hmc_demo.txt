Hamiltonian Monte Carlo: gradient-guided sampling of a correlated Gaussian

  Target: 2-D Gaussian with correlation 0.9 (a narrow diagonal ridge).

                                    true rho 0.9
                   HMC: accept 0.99  recovered rho +0.916  lag-5 autocorr 0.530
  random-walk Metropolis: accept 0.70  recovered rho +0.887  lag-5 autocorr 0.878

  Both are correct in the limit, but HMC's momentum carries it along the ridge in long
  informed sweeps, so its samples decorrelate far faster -- lower autocorrelation means
  more effective samples per step. Random walk shuffles slowly across the narrow ridge.
