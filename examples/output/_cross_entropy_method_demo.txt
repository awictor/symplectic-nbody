Cross-entropy method: derivative-free optimization
====================================================
loop: sample a Gaussian, keep the best (elite) points, re-fit the Gaussian
to them, repeat -- the distribution marches to the optimum and tightens.

benchmark results (all minima are 0):
      function     start f     final f   minimizer
        sphere      75.000     0.00000   [0.0, 0.0, -0.0]
     Rastrigin      13.000     0.00000   [0.0, -0.0]
    Rosenbrock       4.000     0.03320   [0.822, 0.672]

Rastrigin is a field of local minima that traps gradient methods; CEM's
population sampling walks past them to the global optimum at the origin.
