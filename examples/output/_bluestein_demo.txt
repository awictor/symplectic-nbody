  Bluestein rewrites the DFT exponent n*k = (n^2 + k^2 - (k-n)^2)/2, turning the transform
  into a convolution that a power-of-two FFT computes in O(n log n) -- so ANY length becomes
  fast, no zero-padding that would change the transform, no O(n^2) fallback for primes.
