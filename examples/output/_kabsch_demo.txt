Kabsch: the optimal rotation that superimposes two point clouds

  3D exact case (known rotation + translation applied, then recovered):
    RMSD before alignment: 13.5857
    RMSD after Kabsch:     1.77e-05  (recovered the exact transform)

  3D with measurement noise added to the target:
    RMSD after Kabsch:     0.0640  (best possible given the noise)

  Umeyama (adds optimal scale) on a cloud scaled by 1.8x:
    recovered scale: 1.8000  (true 1.8)
    RMSD after alignment: 3.18e-05

  Center both clouds, form the cross-covariance H = P^T Q, take its SVD H = U S V^T, and the
  optimal rotation is R = V U^T -- with a determinant check so it stays a proper rotation,
  never a reflection. Closed form, no iteration; the backbone of structural alignment and
  point-cloud registration.
