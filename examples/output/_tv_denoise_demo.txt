Total-variation denoising: edge-preserving, unlike a blur

  Signal: 4 constant levels + noise (100 samples).

              method   RMSE to truth   plateaus
         noisy input          0.3386         --
      moving average          0.4401         --
          TV denoise          0.2489          5

  TV denoising recovers the flat levels with sharp edges; the moving average rounds
  every step into a ramp, so its error is higher near the jumps.

  Lambda trade-off (more smoothing -> fewer plateaus, lower total variation):
     lambda   total variation   plateaus
        0.5             8.405         18
        1.0             7.534         10
        3.0             6.472          7
        6.0             5.253          4
       12.0             2.853          4
