  Bron-Kerbosch recurses over R (clique so far), P (extenders), X (already-tried); a PIVOT
  in P union X lets it skip candidates that are the pivot's neighbours, since every maximal

  the search. The number of maximal cliques can reach 3^(n/3), so this pruning is essential.
