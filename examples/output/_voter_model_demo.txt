Voter model -- local imitation drives a population to consensus
==============================================================

Consensus probability equals the INITIAL +1 fraction (magnetization martingale):
   initial +1 frac   P(+1 wins) empirical
   ----------------------------------------
    0.20             0.212
    0.40             0.397
    0.50             0.525
    0.60             0.623
    0.80             0.798

Start 70% blue and blue wins ~70% of the time -- outcome is FAIR, not majority-take-all.

Domains coarsen over time (30x30 grid, balanced start):
   step        magnetization   #domains
   ----------------------------------------
      500      +0.062          61
    33500      +0.307          20
    67000      +0.400          21
   100500      +0.402          23
   133500      +0.191          9
   200000      +0.304          18
  reached consensus in 200000 steps -> everyone -1

Magnetization wanders (a martingale) while domains merge; on a finite grid one opinion wins.
