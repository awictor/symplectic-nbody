Stick-breaking (GEM) -- explicit Dirichlet-process weights off a unit stick
==========================================================================

Break Beta(1,alpha) fractions off a length-1 stick: pi_k = beta_k prod(1-beta_j).

Expected weights decay geometrically, E[pi_k] = (1/(1+a))(a/(1+a))^(k-1):
   k     alpha=1     alpha=5
   ------------------------------
   1     0.5000      0.1667
   2     0.2500      0.1389
   3     0.1250      0.1157
   4     0.0625      0.0965
   5     0.0312      0.0804
   6     0.0156      0.0670
   7     0.0078      0.0558

Small alpha -> mass in a few big pieces; large alpha -> many thin, near-equal pieces.

Weights needed to capture 95% of the mass (mean over 500 draws):
   alpha    #weights for 95%
   ------------------------------
     0.3    1.9
     1.0    4.0
     3.0    10.0
    10.0    31.1

One realization (alpha=1, first 10 weights, sum=1.000):
  0.761  0.021  0.085  0.010  0.118  0.001  0.003  0.002  0.000  0.000
  first weight took 76% of the (truncated) mass -- rich-get-richer.
